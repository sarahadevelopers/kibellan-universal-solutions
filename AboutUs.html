Our Story

Built around reliable renal supply

Founded in 2015, Kibellan Universal Solutions has grown from a small renal consumables supplier into a trusted healthcare solutions provider. Our mission has always been to ensure that quality healthcare products are accessible to the institutions that need them most.

We partner with manufacturers, healthcare providers and NGOs to build efficient supply chains that deliver critical dialysis consumables where they're needed most — reliably, ethically, and with the documentation that healthcare procurement demands.
