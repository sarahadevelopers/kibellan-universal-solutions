Clinical Context

Why heparin is used in dialysis

During a hemodialysis session, blood leaves the body and passes through an extracorporeal circuit. Without anticoagulation, the blood would clot in the tubing and dialyzer within minutes.

Prevents Circuit Clotting

Heparin activates antithrombin III, which inhibits thrombin and factor Xa — the primary drivers of clot formation in the dialysis circuit.

Short Half-Life

Heparin's short half-life allows controlled anticoagulation during the session with predictable recovery of normal clotting afterwards.

Prescribed Per Patient

Dose is set by the attending nephrologist based on patient weight, bleeding risk, and dialysis prescription — not a one-size-fits-all dose.

Handling & Storage

How to store heparin correctly

Store at 2–25°C

Controlled room temperature. Avoid areas that exceed 25°C during hot Kenyan months.

Protect from light

Keep vials in original packaging until administration to prevent degradation.

Do not freeze

Freezing damages the heparin molecule and makes the solution unsuitable for use.

Inspect before use

Do not use if the solution is cloudy, discoloured, or contains visible particles.

Common Questions

Procurement FAQ

What is Heparin 5000iu/ml used for in dialysis?

Heparin is given during hemodialysis to prevent blood from clotting in the extracorporeal circuit (bloodlines, dialyzer). It is also used in other procedures requiring short-term anticoagulation. Dosing is prescribed by the attending clinician based on patient weight and bleeding risk.

How should heparin be stored?

Store at controlled room temperature (2°C to 25°C) away from direct sunlight. Do not freeze. Do not use if the solution appears cloudy, contains particles, or if the vial seal is compromised.

Is this a prescription-only medicine?

Yes. Heparin Injection is a prescription-only medicine. It should be administered by or under the supervision of a qualified healthcare professional.

Do you provide cold-chain delivery?

Heparin is stable at controlled room temperature (up to 25°C) and does not strictly require cold-chain during transit under normal Kenyan conditions. For extended transit or unusually hot conditions, we can arrange insulated packaging on request.
