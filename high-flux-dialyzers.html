Common Questions

Procurement FAQ

Which surface area should I choose — 1.8, 2.0 or 2.1 m²?

Surface area determines the total membrane available for solute clearance. 1.8 m² suits smaller adults. 2.0 m² is the most common choice for medium body-weight adults. 2.1 m² is used for larger body-weight patients or when higher clearance is clinically indicated. Follow your nephrologist's prescription for the specific surface area per patient.

What is the difference between high flux and low flux dialyzers?

High flux dialyzers have larger membrane pores, allowing clearance of middle-molecular weight toxins like beta-2-microglobulin. Low flux dialyzers only clear smaller solutes. High flux is generally preferred for chronic hemodialysis patients on long-term treatment.

Are these dialyzers reusable?

These dialyzers are supplied as single-use devices. Some facilities in Kenya reprocess dialyzers for cost reasons, but this requires strict validation and is not recommended by Kibellan Universal Solutions. Follow your facility's reprocessing protocol if applicable.
