Product Overview

Kibellan Universal Solutions supplies universal hemodialysis bloodlines engineered for reliable extracorporeal circulation during dialysis therapy. Each set is manufactured from medical-grade polyvinyl chloride (PVC) and delivered sterile-ready (EO gas sterilized) for immediate clinical use.

The tubing is kink-resistant and color-coded (arterial red, venous blue) to simplify set-up and reduce errors on the dialysis floor. Universal port fitment makes the set compatible with the vast majority of hemodialysis machines in use across Kenyan dialysis centres.

  • Medical-grade PVC — flexible and kink-resistant.
  • EO gas sterilized and individually packed.

Usage & Handling

Universal Bloodlines are supplied sterile and are intended for single use only. Follow your facility's standard operating procedures for dialysis set-up, priming and disposal.

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry environment away from direct sunlight.
  • Do not use if packaging is damaged or compromised.
  • Check expiry date on packaging before use.

Disposal

  • Discard as clinical waste per facility regulations.
  • Never reuse tubing between patients.
