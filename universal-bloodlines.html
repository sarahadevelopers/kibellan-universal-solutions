Clinical Overview

What universal bloodlines do

Bloodlines are the extracorporeal circuit that carries blood between the patient and the dialyzer during a hemodialysis session.

Carry Blood Safely

Arterial and venous lines transport blood to and from the dialyzer at flow rates up to 500 ml/min without collapsing or kinking under vacuum pressure.

Enable Visual Monitoring

Clear medical-grade PVC allows clinicians to see blood flow, detect air, and identify clotting at a glance during the session.

Reduce Set-Up Errors

Arterial lines are colour-coded red and venous lines blue — reducing mix-ups during rush set-up across multiple machines.

Common Questions

Procurement FAQ

Which dialysis machines are universal bloodlines compatible with?

Universal bloodlines use standard universal port fitment that works with the majority of hemodialysis machines in use across Kenyan dialysis centres, including Fresenius, Nipro, B. Braun and Nikkiso systems. If your facility uses a machine with proprietary connectors, contact us — we can source a specific bloodline set.

Are universal bloodlines sterile and single-use?

Yes. Every bloodline set is EO gas sterilized and packaged as a single-use consumable. We do not recommend reprocessing or reusing bloodlines between patients — reuse increases infection risk and is not compliant with good dialysis practice.

Is the price per unit or per box?

Ksh 450 is per set (one complete arterial + venous bloodline). Institutional bulk orders receive tiered pricing — request a quote for quantities above 50 sets and we'll respond within 24 hours.

Do you deliver across Kenya?

Yes. Nairobi and Machakos deliveries typically arrive within 1 working day. Other counties within 2-3 working days depending on location.

Is batch documentation provided?

Yes. Institutional orders are accompanied by batch numbers, manufacturing date, expiry date, and the manufacturer's Certificate of Analysis where applicable. This documentation supports internal procurement audits and regulatory reviews.
