Common Questions

Procurement FAQ

What pharmaceuticals do you supply?

We supply a broad range of branded and generic medicines with a focus on renal care: Heparin, erythropoietin (Wepox), immunosuppressants, phosphate binders, and other nephrology-relevant products. We also source injectables (vaccines, insulin, IV antibiotics), oral antibiotics, cardiovascular medicines, analgesics, and specialty drugs on request. If a specific product isn't in our stock list, we can usually source it through our manufacturer and distributor network.

Are your pharmaceuticals genuine and batch-traceable?

Yes. Every product is sourced through authorised manufacturers and distributors — never through grey-market channels. Institutional orders come with batch numbers, manufacturing dates, expiry dates, and the manufacturer's Certificate of Analysis where applicable.

Do you handle cold-chain pharmaceuticals?

Yes. Temperature-sensitive products (certain injectables, insulin, erythropoietin, some vaccines) are stored in our facility's refrigerated unit and shipped in insulated containers with conditioned gel packs. Cold-chain transport logs are provided for institutional orders that require them.

What is the minimum order quantity?

There is no fixed minimum for institutional buyers. Practically, bulk pricing tiers begin around quantities that make sense for a dialysis unit, clinic or pharmacy — often 50+ units per SKU for standard medicines. Small initial orders are possible if you're trialling us as a supplier.

Can I order on a recurring schedule?

Yes. Hospitals, dialysis centres and pharmacies with predictable monthly consumption can set up recurring delivery cycles. Contact us to schedule your medicine replenishment.

Do you supply pharmacies as well as hospitals?

Yes. We supply registered pharmacies, hospitals, dialysis centres, clinics and NGOs. Pharmacy orders follow standard retail supply terms; hospital and institutional orders follow our full documentation protocol.
