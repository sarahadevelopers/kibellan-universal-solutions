Clinical Context

Why EPO is used in kidney care

Healthy kidneys produce erythropoietin — the hormone that signals the bone marrow to make red blood cells. When kidney function declines, EPO production falls and anaemia develops.

Replaces Natural EPO

Recombinant human erythropoietin mimics the body's own EPO, stimulating red blood cell production in the bone marrow.

Reduces Transfusion Need

Consistent EPO therapy reduces the dependence on blood transfusions in chronic dialysis patients — protecting the vascular access and reducing iron overload risk.

Monitored by Hb Target

Dosing is titrated to target haemoglobin levels by the nephrologist, with regular monitoring to stay within recommended clinical ranges.

Handling & Storage

Correct storage of Wepox

Refrigerate at 2–8°C

Store in a dedicated medical refrigerator — not a domestic kitchen fridge where temperature fluctuates.

Do not freeze

Freezing permanently damages the EPO molecule and renders the product unusable.

Protect from light

Keep vials in their original outer carton until the moment of administration.

Inspect before use

Do not use if the solution is cloudy, discoloured, contains particles, or the vial seal is compromised.

Common Questions

Procurement FAQ

What is Wepox 4000iu used for?

Wepox is a recombinant human erythropoietin (EPO) used to treat anaemia in chronic kidney disease and dialysis patients. It stimulates red blood cell production where kidneys are no longer producing sufficient natural EPO. Dosing is prescribed by the nephrologist based on haemoglobin levels.

Does Wepox require cold-chain storage?

Yes. Wepox must be stored refrigerated at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Protect from light. Inappropriate storage degrades the biological activity of the product. Kibellan Universal Solutions handles Wepox under cold-chain conditions from our facility to your door.

How is Wepox delivered without breaking cold chain?

We pack Wepox in insulated boxes with conditioned gel packs to maintain 2–8°C during transit. Nairobi and Machakos deliveries typically arrive same-day or next-day. For other counties we use priority logistics and monitor transit times. Every shipment carries a temperature log where required for institutional compliance.

Is Wepox a prescription-only medicine?

Yes. Wepox is a prescription-only medicine. It must be administered by or under the supervision of a qualified healthcare professional, and haemoglobin levels should be monitored regularly during treatment.

What documentation comes with Wepox orders?

Every institutional order includes batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date, cold-chain transport records, and the manufacturer's Certificate of Analysis where applicable.
